Teen debit card launch brief

Owner: Geoff · Status: draft for review

Problem

Parents want their teens to learn money habits, but today a teen can only spend with a parent's card. In our spring interviews, six of eight parents said they hand over their own card "and hope".

Proposal

Offer a debit card for teens aged 13 to 17, managed from the parent's app. Parents set weekly spending limits and get an alert for every purchase over the limit they choose.

Declined purchases show the teen a decline codeplain reason and what to do next, so a moment at the till stays calm.

Risks

  • Account takeover through a teen's phone.
  • Merchant categories parents may want to block (gambling, age-restricted goods).

Launch

Pilot with employees' families in Q1, then open to all parent customers.
