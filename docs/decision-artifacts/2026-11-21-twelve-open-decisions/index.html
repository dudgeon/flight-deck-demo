Twelve open decisions, in the order they depend on each other

Update, 1:10 PM: F1 is rewritten after checking upstream: it now asks what the Library should be for. F2 is unchanged. Everything else has gone to the threads building it.

This page brings together three walkthroughs: how demos relate to the Library, how you edit a person in the Hangar, and how messages from participants reach your Inbox. Each decision has its context, pictures, every option, my recommendation and a "Something else" choice. Your picks and notes save as you make them, and I'll pass each one to the thread building it.

Why this order

The demo model comes first because the other two parts build on it. Whether a demo can run live decides whether a cast member needs to know a story, and that changes what you edit on a person and where you cast them. The Inbox comes last because participants who message you on their own get that behaviour from their brief, which you set in the person editor, and scripted messages come from a demo's screenplay.

Lines read "this one shapes that one". Decision 1 feeds 11 because a scripted message only exists in a demo's screenplay. Decisions 2 and 3 feed 9 because casting lists demos by name and gives each cast member that story's facts. Decision 8 feeds 11 because a live participant's urge to reach out sits in their brief, next to their skills.
