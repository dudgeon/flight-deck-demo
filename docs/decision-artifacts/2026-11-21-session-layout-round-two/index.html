One panel that gets out of the way, and where views belong

Two things you asked for. First, direction A refined and shown in motion: what happens to the Session panel as a session moves through its states. Second, the "views in chat" question laid out properly as four options with a mock of each.

Part 1 · Direction A, in motion

The rule behind A is simple. When there is room, the Session panel sits beside the chat. When the canvas opens, the canvas gets that room and the panel folds into one chip in the header that carries the single most important fact. Press a state, or Play, and watch what moves.

What I refined

The chip now changes meaning with the session: plain while idle, a progress count while a workflow runs, and the attention color only when something waits on you. It is the one place to glance.

Card order follows the work

Before a workflow starts, Context leads, because choosing what to work from is the job. Once a workflow runs, its card moves to the top and Context drops below Outputs.

New things announce themselves once

A new output or a participant joining flashes its row once and pulses the chip twice if the panel is folded. Nothing keeps moving after that.

What I left out on purpose

No second rail, no tabs inside the panel, no per-card settings. Those were what made the other directions feel cluttered.

Part 2 · Where should a view appear?

This is the question I asked badly. A "view" is anything drawn from the kit: a form with three choices, a one-record card, a summary, a twenty-row catalog, a board. The question is which of those may be drawn inside the chat transcript, and which only in the canvas. The app today does the first option. The demo previews already do the second.

Option 1 · how the app works today

Canvas only

Catalog setup

Every view opens in the canvas. The chat only says that it did.

  • One place for everything structured. Nothing to build.
  • The chat stays a clean transcript.
  • A three-choice question takes over the whole canvas and hides the document you were reading.
  • Scrolling back, you see that a view was opened but not what you answered.
Option 2 · recommended

Asks in the chat, work in the canvas

Small views that are part of the conversation (a question, a pick-one, a one-record card, a summary) are drawn in the chat. Anything you work in for a while (catalog, board, document) opens in the canvas. Once answered, a chat view freezes into a record of what you chose.

  • The canvas keeps showing your work while you answer a question.
  • The transcript records decisions, which fits attributed turns from participants.
  • One rule the agent can follow: is it an ask, or a workspace?
  • Needs building: the app cannot draw a view inside a message yet.
Option 3

Anything in the chat, expand to canvas

Canvas opens only when you expand something

Every view first appears in the chat at chat width, with a button to open it large.

  • Works without a canvas at all, for narrow windows.
  • A twenty-row table at chat width is cramped, and it scrolls away as the conversation continues.
  • The same live view can exist twice. Which one is current?
  • Most to build and to keep consistent.
Option 4

A still snapshot in the chat, the live view in the canvas

The chat shows a small, non-interactive card of the view as it was at that moment. The live view is always in the canvas.

  • The transcript shows what things looked like when they were made.
  • Never two live copies.
  • Questions still take over the canvas, as in option 1.
  • A snapshot that looks clickable but is not can confuse.
