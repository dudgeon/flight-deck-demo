The whole shape

Three layers. Utility skills give every session its tools and its coordinator. Hero skills are the kinds of PM work, each a workflow with a dataset and views. Templates are what the work is drawn into: widgets in the chat when the person needs to answer or glance, views and documents in the canvas when they work in it.

Hero skills: seven today, four arcs proposed

Each hero skill is a workflow: a SKILL.md, a workflow.json with steps, a dataset, views and a routing block, and a scenario to demo it. The lifecycle research (PR 41, open) proposes regrouping them by what the person sets out to do. None of that regrouping is decided; HS-1 to HS-5 are being asked in the Hero skills and the lifecycle walkthrough.

Fixed checkpoints, free path (HS-2, open)

Today each workflow has a list of steps and a persona with a few invariants that hold at every strictness level (D-130). The proposal keeps steps as a suggested path and adds a short list of checkpoints that pa check can test.

Arc or moveInvariants today (built, in each routing.persona)Proposed checkpoints
Problem discoveryEvery problem cites its sources · never fill a gap with a guess · people by segment, never by nameEvery quote checked against its source · the person chooses what is worth solving
Intent shapingNever write the intent for the person · never score an area deeper than the evidenceEvery gap has an owner or is marked open · the person sets the target depth and says when it is reached
PRD to dependenciesNever set reviewed or locked · every requirement points at its line · never invent a capability · never mark an exploration reviewedEvery dependency has evidence or "could not find" · asks are drafted, a person sends
Delivery statusNever invent a date or capacity · never choose a recovery option · always offer accepting the new dateEvery date change names its cause and source · the update says what is asked of the reader
ReviewEvery finding says who raised it · never accept a finding for the person · context/ is never changedEvery finding points to a line · the author decides each finding
DecideNever decide for the person · record who raised it and why · no padding optionsEach decision records its reason and supersedes, never edits (already enforced by pa set, D-142)

The template data model

Merged in PR 42 (D-138 to D-142). Every template, widget and canvas view below fits into this. The tie between an artifact and its initiative is a stamp inside the file; the registry is read from files on request, so it works from a ZIP and can be swapped for a service later.

Initiative card
projects/<key>.json

ini_ + UUID, a fixed key, name, goal, area, state, and refs to the person's files. Same thing as a project with a goal (IN-1, open; built as recommended).

Shared intent and registers
projects/<key>/intent.md · registers.json

Intent: eight sections, each owned by a stage. Registers: evidence, questions, decisions, ready checks (IN-3, open; built as recommended).

Artifacts, stamped
data/ · artifacts/ · briefs/

art_ id, initiative, template@version, stages (a list), status, by, from. Frontmatter, a JSON block or a pa-artifact script.

The person's files
context/

Listed on the card under refs, never written into.

Three template kinds, one manifest (D-139)

markdown

Headings with one-line guides; sections carry an owner stage. Instance: a stamped .md.

{ "template": "initiative-intent",
  "kind": "markdown",
  "out": "projects/{key}/intent.md",
  "sections": [
    { "id": "problem",
      "owner": "discover",
      "required": true }, … ] }

Shipped: initiative-intent. Proposed: problem brief, intent brief, status update, asks.

records

Schemas plus view-kit specs. Instance: a stamped dataset; its drawn views are renditions, not new artifacts.

{ "template": "initiative-registers",
  "kind": "records",
  "schemas": "schemas/…",
  "views": { "board": … } }

Shipped: initiative-registers. Every workflow's dataset and views are this kind in practice.

page

One self-contained HTML page styled with tokens. Instance: a stamped copy.

<script type="application/json"
  id="pa-artifact">{ "id": "art_…",
  "template": "prd-one-pager@1" }
</script>

The six older artifact pages, read as page templates without a manifest.

How a hero skill uses it (D-142). Find the initiative first; read what exists and what is decided; stamp everything it makes; add to the registers rather than to prose; write only its own stage's intent sections and suggest the rest; extend in its own space. Built in pa; no hero skill follows it yet. That is the next step with the hero-skill refresh.

In-chat widgets

Asks go in the chat. A chat widget is something the person answers or glances at: a form before work starts, a detail card for one record that needs them, or a summary. Drawn with pa render --host chat, at most 720 px, one in the conversation at a time, and folded to a one-line record once the person replies. In the template model a chat widget is a view of a records template, not a template of its own.

Chat and canvas together in a flown scenario (document review): participants speak in the chat, the findings live in the canvas.

In the Hangar each workflow lists its widgets (chat) and views (canvas), drawn from its scenario's data, and they can be edited or added there (D-112).

Canvas templates

Work goes in the canvas. A canvas view is something the person works in: a tree-table catalog reviewed line by line, or a board of pieces of work running at once. Each is a records template's view, redrawn on every pa add and pa set; edits go back through the chat box, and reviewed and locked belong to the person (D-106).

The shared registers, the first records template that belongs to an initiative rather than a workflow: open questions, evidence, decisions (one superseded) and ready checks. Not yet shown on the project page.

Page templates

The six older artifact templates in workspace/templates/artifacts/, now read as the page kind. They are static, token-styled pages with no dataset; D-88 moved them out of the Library.

Dark mode

Views follow the app's theme; the viewer injects the current tokens.
