Messages from people and agents, in the Inbox

Proposal for D-60 to D-62. Participants (simulated people and agents from workspace/participants/) can now reach you outside the session you are looking at. Their message shows up in the Inbox under their own name and mark, the same mark they have in chat. Mock data: the Allowances PRD review.

1. The Inbox with participant messages (recommended design)

A new People tab filters to participant messages. Each message card has a colored left rule and the participant's mark, a chip for AGENT or STAND-IN, their role, and which session it is about. Unread cards have the dot and count toward the sidebar badge. The other Inbox items (an approval, a finished session) stay as they are today.

3. D-60: Which participant messages land in the Inbox?

Recommended

A. Messages meant for you

  • They reach out on their own (Mara's note)
  • A follow-up after the call ended (Risk Review)
  • Replies while the host is relaying the call: stay in chat only
The Inbox stays a short list of things a person would message you about. A call's replies already make the session unread, which the Inbox lists today.

B. Also every call reply you missed

  • Everything in A
  • Each participant reply in a session you are not looking at
Nothing gets missed, but a five-person review puts five cards and a session card in the Inbox for one piece of work.

C. Every participant message

  • Everything, even in the session you have open
The Inbox becomes a full log of who said what. Noisy, and it repeats the chat.

4. D-61: Where does a reply go?

Recommended

A. Through the host session

Your reply is posted in the host session as @mara-okafor …. Aviator relays it and shows her answer as her turn. One conversation holds everything, the same as when you @mention someone in chat.

B. Straight to their session

Your reply goes to Mara's own background session. Faster, but the host session doesn't see it, so Aviator can lose track of what was agreed.

C. No inline reply

The card only has Open conversation. Simplest, and you always reply in context, but every answer costs a page change.

5. D-62: How do participants decide to message you?

Scripted (demos)A screenplay step drops a message at a set point, for example inbox: mara-okafor after the call ends. Same every run, good for recordings.
+
Live (session-backed)The participant's background session can post with the existing Inbox tool when its brief says to, for example "follow up if a blocker is still open when the call ends". Their file gets a reach_out line saying when.
=
Recommended: both, scripted firstSame as the call-in decision earlier: build the scripted path so demos work today, then let live participants post with the same card.

Under the hood (for the production team): participant messages reuse the Inbox's existing agent-posted items (/v1/inbox/items, the sys_user_inbox_post tool) with a new participant field, so no new store is needed.
