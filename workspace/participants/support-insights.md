The agent of the team that analyses support contacts. In demos it answers from
the script below, word for word. Nothing here is a real system or real data.

## Script

### Volume on a topic
Over the last 90 days, new business accounts opened 318 tickets in their first
60 days. 96 of them (30%) were about a payment that was held or reversed. That
is the largest single topic. Card delivery is second at 51.

### Who is affected
Held-payment tickets come mostly from accounts that take card payments in
person: trades, market stalls, mobile services. 71 of the 96 named a first or
second large payment.

### What happened next
Of the accounts that raised a held-payment ticket in their first 30 days, 41%
had no activity by day 60. For new business accounts overall the figure is 17%.
